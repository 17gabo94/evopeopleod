# EvoPeople Professional Website Specification

## Executive Summary

EvoPeople OD Consulting requires a professional, visually sophisticated business website that establishes the firm as a credible organizational development and talent consulting partner. The website will serve as the primary digital presence, attract prospective clients, communicate capabilities clearly, and create a pathway to client engagement.

The platform must embody the firm's commitment to strategic thinking, professional excellence, and measurable business impact while adhering to strict quality, mobile-responsive design, and SEO/SEM optimization principles established in the project constitution.

---

## 1. Business Context & Objectives

### 1.1 Business Model
EvoPeople OD Consulting is a professional services firm specializing in organizational development, talent management, leadership development, and strategic HR advisory. The firm works with mid-to-large organizations seeking to strengthen organizational capabilities, develop leadership, and drive culture change.

### 1.2 Primary Objectives

**Establish Credibility**
- Position EvoPeople as a trustworthy, professional consulting partner
- Communicate 15+ years of expertise and proven track record
- Demonstrate deep knowledge of OD, talent strategy, and organizational transformation

**Attract Prospective Clients**
- Reach decision-makers (Chief HR Officers, VP of Talent, organizational leaders)
- Clearly communicate the value of consulting engagements
- Create multiple pathways for prospects to initiate conversations

**Communicate Value**
- Translate technical OD concepts into business impact language
- Show how the firm's services address real organizational challenges
- Demonstrate strategic thinking and practical execution capability

**Enable Business Development**
- Support future client acquisition and consulting engagements
- Establish a scalable foundation for service expansion
- Enable integration of case studies, client testimonials, and thought leadership

### 1.3 Target Audiences
- **Primary**: Chief Human Resources Officers, VP Talent & Organization, Organization Development Directors, HR Business Partners at mid-to-large organizations
- **Secondary**: Executive Coaches, Organizational Leaders, Business Transformation Leaders
- **Exploratory**: Prospective Partners, Referral Sources

## 3. User Scenarios & Information Flows

### 3.1 Primary User Journey: Prospect Discovery & Engagement

**Scenario 1: Executive Researching External OD Support**
1. Search for "organizational development consultant" or "talent strategy firm" (SEO/SEM)
2. Land on homepage
3. Assess credibility and understand value proposition (30 seconds)
4. Navigate to "Our Approach" to understand methodology
5. Explore specific services relevant to their challenge
6. Contact firm to discuss their situation

**Success Criteria**: Clear navigation, compelling value proposition, easy contact path

**Scenario 2: Referred Prospect**
1. Arrive via LinkedIn referral or recommendation
2. Quickly confirm this is a legitimate, professional firm
3. Explore specific service areas relevant to their need
4. Contact to arrange consultation

**Success Criteria**: Professional appearance, clear service descriptions, easy contact

**Scenario 3: HR Leader Evaluating Multiple Firms**
1. Compare EvoPeople's approach to other consulting firms
2. Assess experience and capability depth
3. Evaluate engagement model and philosophy
4. Determine fit and initiate conversation

**Success Criteria**: Clear differentiation, credible evidence of expertise, accessible contact process

### 3.2 Secondary User Journeys

**Scenario 4: Partner or Referral Source**
- Explore to understand what the firm offers (for potential referrals)
- Contact to discuss partnership or referral relationship

**Scenario 5: Job Seeker or Career Explorer** (unintended)
- May land on site looking for employment opportunities
- Should gracefully communicate this is a consulting services site
- Can link to LinkedIn for employment inquiries

## 4. Visual Experience & Brand Requirements

### 4.1 Brand Identity Principles

The website must communicate:
- **Technical Expertise**: Deep knowledge of OD, talent strategy, and organizational transformation
- **Professional Reliability**: Consistent, trustworthy, detail-oriented
- **Clear Communication**: Explains complex concepts simply; avoids jargon
- **Thoughtful Problem-Solving**: Strategic thinking combined with practical execution
- **Business-Oriented Mindset**: Focused on client outcomes, not theoretical frameworks
- **Quality & Attention to Detail**: Polished, carefully composed, professionally executed

### 4.2 Design Approach

**DO:**
- Use sophisticated typography with careful hierarchy
- Apply consistent, purposeful color system
- Employ strategic whitespace for clarity and focus
- Create distinctive visual identity (not generic template)
- Use imagery that communicates professionalism and strategic thinking
- Design for accessibility and clarity first, aesthetics second
- Ensure every visual element serves a purpose

**DON'T:**
- Use generic portfolio templates or agency themes
- Apply excessive gradients, animations, or decorative elements
- Rely on card-based layouts without differentiation
- Use stock photography that feels generic or inauthentic
- Add animations that distract from content
- Overcomplicate navigation or user flows

### 4.3 Visual Design Specifications

#### **Typography System**
- Primary Typeface: Sophisticated, professional sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Poppins, or similar; no Comic Sans, no overly stylized fonts)
- Secondary Typeface: Complementary serif or carefully chosen sans-serif for contrast
- Hierarchy: Clear distinction between heading levels, body text, and metadata
- Readability: Minimum 16px body text, sufficient line-height (1.5-1.6), high contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)

#### **Color System**
- Primary Color: Professional, confident tone (suggest: deep navy, forest green, or sophisticated slate)
- Secondary Color: Complementary accent for CTAs and important elements
- Neutral Palette: Carefully chosen grays for background, borders, metadata
- Semantic Colors: Clear green for success, red for errors, amber for warnings
- Avoid: Bright gradients, excessive color variation, trendy but short-lived color choices

#### **Spacing & Layout**
- Modular spacing scale (e.g., 8px baseline: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96px)
- Generous whitespace to support readability and visual clarity
- Consistent margins and padding across components
- Responsive grid system (e.g., 12-column mobile/tablet/desktop)

#### **Imagery & Visual Communication**
- Photography: Professional, authentic images if used; avoid generic stock photos
- Icons: Consistent, minimal icon system (if used at all)
- Diagrams/Illustrations: Strategic visuals that aid understanding, not decoration
- Avoid: Excessive animation, auto-playing video, intrusive pop-ups

### 4.4 Component Library

Components must support the constitution's quality-first and mobile-responsive principles:

**Core Components**:
- Navigation bar (fixed/sticky, responsive menu)
- Hero section
- Card layouts (if used) with clear purpose differentiation
- Call-to-action buttons (primary, secondary, tertiary)
- Form elements (text, email, textarea, select, submit)
- Breadcrumb navigation
- Footer
- Service section templates
- Image galleries (if needed)
- Testimonial/quote displays (future)

## 6. SEO & SEM Requirements

Per EvoPeople Web Platform Constitution, SEO & SEM optimization is a core principle:

### 6.1 SEO Fundamentals
- [ ] Semantic HTML structure (proper heading hierarchy, meaningful elements)
- [ ] Meta descriptions for all pages (compelling, under 160 characters)
- [ ] Open Graph tags for social sharing
- [ ] Structured data markup (schema.org: Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, WebPage)
- [ ] XML sitemap
- [ ] robots.txt configuration
- [ ] Internal linking strategy (contextual links between related services)
- [ ] URL structure: readable, hierarchical, consistent (e.g., /services/leadership-development/)

### 6.2 Content Optimization
- [ ] Target keywords: "organizational development," "talent management," "leadership development," "OD consulting," "HR transformation," etc.
- [ ] Page titles optimized for search and user (50-60 characters)
- [ ] Body content includes targeted keywords naturally (no keyword stuffing)
- [ ] Heading hierarchy supports both UX and SEO
- [ ] Image alt text (descriptive, keyword-relevant where appropriate)
- [ ] Mobile-first indexing compatibility

### 6.3 Technical SEO
- [ ] SSL/HTTPS (mandatory)
- [ ] Fast page load times (Core Web Vitals: LCP <2.5s, FID <100ms, CLS <0.1)
- [ ] Mobile responsiveness (mobile-first design)
- [ ] Proper URL canonicalization
- [ ] Structured data validation (using Schema.org)
- [ ] No broken links or 404s

### 6.4 SEM Readiness
- [ ] Clear brand messaging for paid search targeting
- [ ] Compelling ad copy on every key page
- [ ] Landing pages optimized for conversion (clear value prop, CTA, trust signals)
- [ ] Google Analytics 4 setup
- [ ] Conversion tracking (form submissions, CTA clicks)
- [ ] UTM parameter strategy

## 9. Assumptions & Constraints

### 9.1 Assumptions
- The existing Pagina OD.md content is accurate and complete for initial launch
- Email or contact form backend is available/will be configured separately
- No e-commerce, payment processing, or complex backend systems required
- Initial launch does not include case studies, client testimonials, or team pages
- Domain/hosting available (or will be configured)
- SSL certificate available

### 9.2 Constraints
- Static or low-complexity backend (supports scalability and cost-efficiency)
- No authentication or user login required for initial version
- Responsive design required for all browsers (no "desktop only")
- WCAG AA accessibility non-negotiable (per quality-first principle)
- SEO optimization mandatory from day one (per constitution)

### 9.3 Out of Scope (Initial Launch)
- Client portal or content access system
- Payment processing or booking system
- Team member pages (unless included in content)
- Detailed case studies (until clients consent to sharing)
- Multi-language support (clarify if needed)
- Blog/insights section (can be added post-launch)
- Community forum or discussion features
- Mobile app
